About Us

Experts in Manu: more than three decades of real experience

Marco Rozas, founder of Manu Adventures, discovered Manu in the early 1990s and found in the jungle not only a destination, but a life mission. Since then, he has dedicated more than 33 years to exploring, understanding, and protecting this extraordinary territory.

We created the 3-, 4-, 5-, 6-, and 7-day programs that today define the way Manu is explored. In addition, we have contributed to the training of 70% of the guides currently working in the region.

Our team—guides, drivers, motorcyclists, and cooks—not only has experience, but also a deep connection to this place, as many of them were born and raised here.

The local families who work with Manu Adventures come from grandparents, parents, and now children. We are proud that these generations have learned about responsible tourism, walking hand in hand with us.

Traveling with Manu Adventures means discovering Manu accompanied by those who know it, respect it, and consider it their home.
