Private tours: the jungle at your own pace

All our programs can be arranged as private tours, whether for individual travelers, families, or groups of friends. This option allows you to choose your departure date and experience Manu with complete freedom, accompanied by a team dedicated exclusively to your experience.

Experience the Amazon in a Unique Way

A private trip offers the privilege of exploring the Amazon with greater flexibility, depth, and personal connection.

Accompanied by specialized guides, you will be able to explore unique landscapes, observe wildlife, and enjoy an unforgettable experience in the heart of the Peruvian Amazon.
