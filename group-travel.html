GROUP TRAVEL

Group trips to Manu: an authentic experience in the Peruvian Amazon

At Manu Adventures, we offer tours to Manu National Park designed to suit your travel style. The small group option has been created to offer a deep, respectful, and authentic connection with the Amazon rainforest of Peru.

Group travel: small, authentic, and personal

Our 3-, 4-, and 5-day programs have confirmed departures two or three times a week, allowing you to easily join a group on convenient dates. In some cases, these departures offer preferential rates.

For the more in-depth 6- and 7-day programs in the Manu Reserved Zone, we require a minimum of 3 passengers, although we also have confirmed departures on specific dates.

Our groups are always small, with an average of 6 to 8 travelers, and often fewer. This allows us to preserve the essence of the trip: an intimate, immersive experience in harmony with the natural rhythm of the jungle.
