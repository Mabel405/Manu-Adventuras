What makes our Manu tours unique

Choosing a trip to Manu National Park also means choosing how you want to discover the jungle. At Manu Adventures, we have designed our programs with a different vision: to allow travelers to experience Manu in depth, without rushing, and in true connection with nature.

¿What sets us apart from other operators?

We are the only operator that spends the first night in the Cloud Forest, at 1,400 meters above sea level. This decision, the result of decades of experience, completely transforms the beginning of the trip. Instead of leaving at dawn and traveling long distances in a hurry, we advance gradually, exploring the high Andean landscapes and the extraordinary transition forest, where the Andes merge with the Amazon. Sleeping in this privileged ecosystem, surrounded by the sound of rivers and birdsong, allows for natural acclimatization and an early connection with the essence of Manu. Sunrise in the Cloud Forest is not just the beginning of a new day: it is the beginning of a true immersion in the jungle.

¿How does this experience enhance the rest of the trip?

Thanks to this unique design, the second day becomes a day of active and authentic exploration. We descend into the lowland jungle with time and freedom, with the possibility of experiences such as downhill biking, rafting on Amazonian rivers, and exploring remote places such as Pongo de Koñec, a natural enclave known only to us. Each day has been designed to reveal Manu progressively, allowing for real encounters with biodiversity and moments that cannot be replicated in conventional itineraries.

Traveling to Manu is a safe, accessible, and deeply transformative experience, designed so that you can experience the jungle with confidence and serenity.
